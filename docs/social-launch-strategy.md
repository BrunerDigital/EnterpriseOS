## Paid Launch Structure

Start small until analytics and pixels are confirmed.

- Meta: traffic or lead campaign, square and story assets, warm audiences first.
- LinkedIn: single-image sponsored content for agencies, consultants, franchise operators, and service business owners.
- Google Search: branded terms plus high-intent phrases like white-label CRM, marketing automation platform, agency CRM, and business operating system.
- Retargeting: visitors who viewed the landing page but did not submit the demo form.

Starter budget suggestion:

- Day 1-3: $25-$50/day per channel while validating conversion tracking.
- Day 4-7: increase only on channels with booked walkthroughs or qualified demo requests.

## Measurement

- Track source and UTM values in Supabase leads.
- Measure click-to-demo-request conversion.
- Measure demo request to booked walkthrough.
- Review post engagement after 2 hours, 6 hours, and 24 hours.
- Repost the best-performing angle as the next paid creative concept.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim Stripe, Twilio, SendGrid, Mailgun, Google Calendar, Meta, Google Business Profile, or OpenAI are live for production customers until credentials and webhooks are connected.
- Position current integrations as structured placeholders or connection-ready modules.
- For paid traffic, confirm privacy and consent language before collecting SMS or email opt-ins at scale.
